    RocketStack: Level-aware deep recursive ensemble learning architecture

    RocketStack departs from traditional flat ensemble stacking by utilizing a deep, recursive architecture.

    Why RocketStack?

    Traditional stacking combines predictions in a single meta-learning stage. RocketStack instead builds successive levels: out-of-fold probability features are propagated into deeper representations, feature growth is controlled, and the active learner pool is refined between levels.

    • Recursive stacking: probability features move through successive meta-learning levels.
    • Level-aware pruning: the blurrization heuristic introduces controlled score perturbation and removes weaker learners between levels.
    • Intermediate feature processing: stochastic feature elimination, autoencoder reduction, and attention-based selection are supported.
    • Diverse learner pool: scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost models participate in the ensemble.
    • Reusable models: the public API provides familiar fit, predict, predict_proba, and score workflows.
